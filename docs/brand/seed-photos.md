# Sample article photos

The photos in `public/seed/`, used by `pnpm db:seed`'s sample articles (FER-010, FER-044), its catalog covers (FER-043) and its home banners (FER-020). Every one is free for commercial use with no attribution legally required, under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) or the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/); the credit is kept here anyway. The first eleven were resized to at most 1200 px on their long side, and the bubble gun was cropped to 4:3. A `-phone.jpg` is its photo's centre cropped square, a catalog cover's phone image (FER-043), or, for a home banner's phone photo (FER-020), its centre cropped to a portrait 4:5 with `sips`: a cover has one alt text, so its phone image shows the same thing as its computer one. The twenty-eight added for FER-044 (Lucas approved more free photos on 2026-11-18) were taken through Unsplash's own download link, which answers only for a photo under the Unsplash License (an Unsplash+ one is refused), or from Pexels's image server, checked to be JPEG images and resized to at most 1600 px on their long side. A seeded article may reuse a photo (its gallery, or a variant of the same piece); the words the seed writes about a photo describe what it shows, never a material nobody checked (`src/server/db/seed-articles.ts`).
